[Backup · if asked how we differ] How is this different from what exists? BMC's dashboard shows live site readings and threshold alerts. SAFAR forecasts air quality at city level. Vendor devices can switch on sprinklers, but only after a threshold is crossed. None of them checks whether a sensor can be trusted, finds sites with no sensor, or tells a site when dusty work is safe. DustTrace does all of that on top of their data, not instead of it.
