[1:25 · 25 s] Why does this matter? Look at last winter. Deonar, Shivaji Nagar, Malad, Mazgaon, Sewri: all averaged between 70 and 80 micrograms of PM2.5. The national annual standard is 40. That's nearly double. A 2024 Lancet Planetary Health study linked around thirty-three thousand deaths a year across ten Indian cities, Mumbai included, to short-term PM2.5 exposure. And last October, 19 Mumbai stations recorded their worst PM2.5 since January. Winter is when the monsoon stops washing the dust out of the air.
