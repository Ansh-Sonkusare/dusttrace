[Backup · show if asked "where's the IoT?"] Here's what most people don't know: Mumbai already has an IoT network for this problem. In 2023, BMC issued dust guidelines and the High Court stepped in. In January 2025, the court ordered site-level monitoring instead of blanket ward bans. Since June 2025, every active site must install a BMC-approved PM2.5 and PM10 sensor, with an LED board at the gate, reporting every 30 seconds to BMC's Command and Control Centre. By November, 662 were installed. But only about 400 actually reached the dashboard. So when someone asks "where's the IoT?", this is it. The hardware exists. The intelligence on top of it doesn't.
