[5:25 · 30 s] Our second unique element uses something every site already has: the LED board the High Court made compulsory. Next to the PM readings, we show a Dust Score out of 100. It combines how trustworthy the site's sensor is, how often spikes trace back to it, whether it followed its Safe-Work Windows, verified citizen reports, and inspection history. There's a QR code on the board. A resident scans it and reports dust in thirty seconds, already tagged to that exact site. And the score has consequences. We propose that high scorers earn fewer routine inspections and low scorers go to the top of the list. That rewards compliance instead of only punishing violations. The weights and incentives are proposals to design with BMC.
