[1:55 · 25 s] So here's our idea. Every tool in Mumbai today asks one question: who polluted? And it asks it after the air has already turned bad. We flip it. DustTrace asks: when and where will dust hit people tomorrow, and what can we change on site today to stop it? That gives us three layers: Predict, Prevent, Prove. Prevent is the part nobody does today, and it's where our two unique elements live.
