[6:50 · 30 s] Now the money. Developers are already paying one to two lakh rupees per sensor. Across twelve hundred sites, that's twelve to twenty-four crore of hardware the city isn't using well. Running DustTrace city-wide costs an estimated fifty to sixty lakh a year, plus about five lakh once for two hundred of our own nodes. Compare that with five to ten crore just to put sensors on the five hundred unsensored sites, which still wouldn't audit or forecast anything. And the funding already exists: Greater Mumbai has received over 938 crore in NCAP air-quality grants. We'd sell per-ward licences to BMC and data access to MPCB. Developers pay for Safe-Work Windows and a verified Dust Score, because following a window costs nothing and a stop-work notice halts the whole site.
