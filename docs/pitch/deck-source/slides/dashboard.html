[5:55 · 35 s] This is our working web prototype, not a mock-up. It's what a BMC ward officer in K-East opens at 8:40 in the morning. One: a briefing in plain words. A 78 percent chance PM10 crosses 100 tomorrow morning, six sites in Avoid windows, seven sensors we don't trust. Two: the dust weather map. Each orange shape is a forecast plume from a construction site, drifting with the wind over the school and the hospital. Three: press play and you watch tomorrow happen, hour by hour, as plumes grow and sites switch between Go and Avoid. Four: the Needs you queue. Every alert comes with the fix as a button: turn on the mist at Site B, tell Site A's manager they're demolishing in the wrong window, send a flat sensor to inspection, warn the school. Five: green diamonds are our own nodes, and the teal markers are BEST buses carrying sensors past the sites.
