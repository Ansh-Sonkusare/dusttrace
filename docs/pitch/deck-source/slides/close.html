Thank you. We'd love your questions. Q&A PREP: Why only dust, when the theme is AQI? The brief asks for a specific pollution source, so we went deep on Mumbai's biggest one. It's also the only source with a court-mandated sensor network, and the only one where the timing of emissions can be moved. The engines extend to any source that stays in one place: RMC plants, bakeries, waste burning, road dust (appendix A6). Why would developers follow Safe-Work Windows? Following one costs nothing; it raises their Dust Score and lowers their chance of a stop-work notice. BMC could also make it a condition of approval. Can you really switch on sprinklers? Some BMC-approved monitors, such as Oizom AQBot, already have relay outputs for sprinklers and mist cannons. Sites without relays get the alert on WhatsApp instead. Where's the IoT? The BMC site-sensor network and CAAQMS stations (appendix slide A1; architecture A3, models A4); plus our own DustTrace Nodes at schools, housing societies and unsensored areas (slide 7). Why not smog towers, or filters on buses? The evidence says they barely move city air. Delhi's pollution committee told the NGT its smog towers were ineffective: IIT Bombay measured about 50% cleaner air within 20 m but only 12 to 13% at 100 to 200 m, at Rs 25 crore to build and Rs 15 lakh a month to run each tower. A roof filter on a UK bus removed 65 g of particulates in 100 days; even 2,500 such buses would remove about 588 kg of PM10 a year, against Mumbai's roughly 87,000 tonnes. So we put sensors on buses, not filters, and capture dust at the source with mist curtains. Isn't this just diverting dust? No. We fix the cause at the site, capture dust with forecast-triggered mist curtains and green screens, and protect people indoors at downwind schools (slide 9). How do you get BMC data? It's simulated for now; we've requested an RTI and vendor samples, and there's an MoU path. The product still works on public data without it (appendix A2). How is this different from BMC's dashboard? See appendix A5: their dashboard shows readings; we add prevention, trust checks, discovery and ranking. Is construction really the main source? BMC's plan ranks it first. Estimates vary by study: SAFAR puts it at about 8% city-wide, and NEERI found construction plus natural dust at 27% at Dharavi. Near active sites it dominates, and that's where we act.
