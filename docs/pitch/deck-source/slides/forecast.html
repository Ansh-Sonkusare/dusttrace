[Backup · show if asked about the models] Forecast asks one question: will PM10 cross the national 24-hour standard of 100 micrograms in the next 24, 48 or 72 hours? We train a gradient-boosted model per station on four years of real data, and it has to beat two baselines: "tomorrow looks like today", and SAFAR's own forecast.
