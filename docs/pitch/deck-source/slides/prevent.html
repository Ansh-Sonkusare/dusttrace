Fixes follow BMC's 28-point dust guidelines, which already include water spraying, covered transport and green screens (MCGM 2023; Hindustan Times, 29 Mar 2023).

[4:10 · 40 s] Rescheduling is only one lever. DustTrace also finds the cause and sends the fix. A work-hour spike of coarse dust, where PM10 jumps far more than PM2.5, points to dry excavation, so the site is told to spray water or shift to its safe window. Spikes at the gate at truck times point to track-out: wheel wash and covered loads. Satellite and photos catch uncovered debris. And a dry, windy forecast means pre-wet the site. We also don't just divert dust; we capture and clean it. A mist curtain along the boundary switches on from the forecast, green screens on the hoardings trap coarse dust, and when a school is downwind, it's alerted to close windows and run classroom purifiers.
