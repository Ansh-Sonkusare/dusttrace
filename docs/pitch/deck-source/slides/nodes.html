[3:00 · 35 s] Where Mumbai has no sensors, we add our own. A DustTrace Node is an ESP32 board, a laser dust sensor and a humidity sensor in a weatherproof box. It costs about 2,700 rupees, against one to two lakh for a site monitor. Fixed nodes go where people live and breathe: housing societies next to sites, schools and hospitals downwind. And the same node, with GPS, rides on BEST buses. Mumbai runs 2,792 of them, 1,324 electric, and they pass construction sites every day, so we get readings at sites with no sensor, and a check on sites whose own sensor says everything is fine. They measure real exposure. They prove whether a Safe-Work Window actually cut the dust at the school. And they give every developer's sensor an independent neighbour to be checked against. We calibrate them against CPCB stations, and the official site monitors stay the compliance record.
