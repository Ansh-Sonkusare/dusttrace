[Backup · if asked how data becomes decisions] This is how we use the data. Every input becomes a decision, and every decision has someone who acts on it. Station history and weather go into a forecast model, which flags tomorrow's risky hours. Wind and a map of schools and hospitals go into a plume model, which gives each site its Safe-Work Window. Each sensor is checked against its neighbours and the station, which gives a trust score. Satellite images and citizen photos reveal sites with no sensor. And everything combines into one ranked list that becomes the officer's route for the day.
