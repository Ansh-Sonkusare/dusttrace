[Backup · worked example] Here's one site, start to finish. The forecast gives a 78 percent chance of a spike between seven and eleven. The plume model shows the wind carrying Site A's dust toward a school, so Site A gets Avoid: demolition moves to the afternoon and misting starts at five-thirty. Meanwhile, its sensor reads 42 while the station downwind reads 140, so its trust score drops to 28 and Site A goes to the top of the inspection list. These numbers are illustrative; the prototype produces the real ones.
