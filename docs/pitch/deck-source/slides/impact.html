Prototype screen on simulated demo data. Targets are our assumptions to validate, not results; health gains via WHO AirQ+.

[7:20 · 25 s] We don't want to claim impact; we want to measure it, and the product is built to do that. This screen shows the pilot view on simulated data: K-East against two control wards, and every case closes only on a measured drop. These are our pilot targets: at least double the violations found per inspection, 80 percent precision on flagged sensors, 70 percent recall on unsensored sites, a forecast that beats persistence, twenty percent fewer people downwind during dusty work, and a 10 percent PM10 drop on high-risk days compared with control wards. We'll measure it with a proper difference-in-differences design over one winter. The people who benefit first are residents next to sites, and the construction workers who breathe this dust all day.
