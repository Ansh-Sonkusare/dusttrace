[Backup · a day with DustTrace] Here's how it all fits together in one day. At six in the evening, the forecast flags a likely morning spike in K-East. Five minutes later, twelve upwind sites get their Safe-Work Windows on WhatsApp, and demolition moves to the afternoon. At five-thirty the next morning, mist cannons at those sites switch on through their sensor relays, ninety minutes before the risky window. At nine, the ward officer opens a ranked list: two sites ignored their window, one sensor is flatlined, and two sites have no sensor at all. At eleven, a resident scans a QR code and reports uncovered debris. By evening, outcomes are logged, scores update and the models retrain. The numbers here show the flow; they aren't results.
